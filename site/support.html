Other ways the school invites support

Leave a legacy

The donate page suggests a beneficiary form on a retirement account or insurance policy, a bequest in a will or trust, or naming Jordan Glen on a bank or investment account.

Retirement funds

If you are at least 70½, the school says you can contribute directly from an IRA. They ask you to contact your financial advisor to start a qualified charitable distribution.

Things, not only money

Non-cash gifts can be deductible. The school asks you to check before discarding a computer, books, furniture, or other goods that might be useful. They also accept school and office supplies, and household items such as paper towels, toilet paper, paper plates, and plastic cutlery. A receipt for tax purposes is available on request.
