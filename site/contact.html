How to get here

From Gainesville, head west on Archer Road and turn left onto 154th Street at the Jordan Glen School sign. The homepage describes the drive as eight miles west of Gainesville. The contact page says about ten minutes west of Gainesville, just east of Archer.

Schedule a tour Open in Google Maps

There is no separate tour form. The contact page says to call or send a message. The button uses the school’s request-information form.

Employment

The FAQ says there are currently no new teacher positions anticipated. You may send a resume with your qualifications and experience for the school to keep on file. There is no separate jobs page on the current site. Send questions through the information form or call the office.
