Activity fee: $400 per student

The fee includes most books loaned for the school year, most field trips, pizza lunch on Fridays, and a softcover school yearbook.

It does not include extracurricular activities, clubs, some special trips and events, student photocopier and printer use, or school supplies. The 2026–27 supply list is linked from the calendar page.
